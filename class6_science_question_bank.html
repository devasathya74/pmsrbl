Answer: (c) Observation

C SHORT & LONG ANSWER QUESTIONS

Q1. Write the steps of the scientific method in order. (3 Marks)

Ans:
  1. Observe something interesting or not understood.
  2. Ask a question.
  3. Guess a possible answer (Hypothesis).
  4. Test the guess through experiments or more observations.
  5. Analyse the results.

Q2. Why is science not merely memorising facts? (2 Marks)

Ans: Science involves observation, questioning, testing ideas, and analysing results, not just memorising information.

Q3. Explain the scientific method with a daily-life example. (5 Marks)

Ans: The scientific method is a step-by-step process. First, we observe a problem. Then we ask a question, make a possible guess, test it through experiments or checks, and analyse results.
Example: If a pen stops writing, we first check if the ink is finished. If ink is present, we check if the tip is dried or clogged. This is scientific thinking in everyday life.

DIVERSITY IN THE LIVING WORLD

Plants, Animals & Habitat

Q1. Differentiate between Taproot and Fibrous root. (3 Marks)

Feature Taproot Fibrous root
Structure Has one main root with side roots Bunch of thin, similar-sized roots
Venation Link Reticulate venation (mostly Dicot) Parallel venation (mostly Monocot)
Examples Mustard, Hibiscus, Mango Grass, Wheat, Maize

Q2. Differentiate between Monocot and Dicot plants. (3 Marks)

Monocot Dicot
One cotyledon in seed Two cotyledons in seed
Fibrous roots Taproot system
Parallel leaf venation (e.g. Maize, Grass) Reticulate leaf venation (e.g. Chickpea, Mustard)

Q3. Explain Habitat and Adaptation with examples. (5 Marks)

Ans:
• Habitat: The place where an organism lives and gets food, water, air, and shelter.
• Adaptation: Special features that help organisms survive in their specific environment.
• Example 1: Cactus has a thick fleshy green stem to store water and reduce water loss in deserts.
• Example 2: Deodar / Conifers in mountain regions have a conical shape and sloping branches so snow slides off easily.
Chapter 3

MINDFUL EATING: A PATH TO A HEALTHY BODY

Nutrition & Health

Q1. Explain Nutrient Types, Functions & Sources. (5 Marks)

  • Energy-Giving Foods: Carbohydrates and Fats (Rice, Wheat, Potato, Oils, Ghee).
  • Body-Building Foods: Proteins for growth and repair (Pulses, Milk, Eggs, Paneer).
  • Protective Nutrients: Vitamins and Minerals for disease defense (Fruits, Green Vegetables).
  • Roughage (Dietary Fibre): Helps in bowel movement and removal of undigested waste.
  • Water: Essential for nutrient absorption and excretion.

Q3. What are Food Tests for Starch, Fat and Protein? (3 Marks)

• Starch Test: Add dilute Iodine solution → turns Blue-Black.
• Fat Test: Rub food on clean paper → an Oily Translucent Patch appears.
• Protein Test: Copper sulphate + Caustic soda solution → turns Violet.

Q4. What are Millets and Food Miles? (2 Marks)

• Millets: Nutri-cereals (Jowar, Bajra, Ragi) rich in fibre, minerals, and vitamins.
• Food Miles: The distance food travels from where it is grown to where it is consumed. We should prefer locally grown food to minimize food miles.
