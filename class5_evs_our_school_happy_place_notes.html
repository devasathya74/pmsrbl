Chapter Summary & Key Concepts (अध्याय सारांश)

Joyful Learning Environment

English Summary

A school is not just a building of brick and mortar; it is a second home where children spend valuable years learning, playing, and building friendships. A happy school is safe, clean, inclusive, and green.

This chapter teaches students how to observe their school campus closely, map its physical layout, and work collaboratively in Student Action Teams (Water Watchers, Waste Warriors, Green Guardians, Electricity Savers, and Traffic Trackers). It introduces the Green School Concept, where students learn water conservation, electricity saving, waste segregation (Green and Blue dustbins), composting, and emergency safety rules.

सरल हिंदी सारांश

विद्यालय केवल ईंट-पत्थरों से बना भवन नहीं है, बल्कि यह हमारा दूसरा घर है जहाँ हम नए ज्ञान, संस्कार, खेल और दोस्ती सीखते हैं। एक आनंदमय विद्यालय वह होता है जो सुरक्षित, स्वच्छ, हरा-भरा और सभी बच्चों के लिए स्वागतयोग्य हो।

यह पाठ विद्यार्थियों को अपने विद्यालय का नक्शा (मैप) बनाना, साफ-सफाई रखना और विभिन्न विद्यार्थी दलों (जल प्रहरी, स्वच्छता सेनानी, हरित संरक्षक, ऊर्जा रक्षक व यातायात निरीक्षक) के रूप में जिम्मेदारी उठाना सिखाता है। इसमें 'ग्रीन स्कूल' की अवधारणा, कचरे का सही अलगाव (गीला और सूखा कचरा), पानी व बिजली की बचत, तथा आपातकालीन सुरक्षा के नियम सिखाए गए हैं।

Student Action Teams & Their Roles (विद्यार्थी अन्वेषक दल)

5 Core Teams

To make the school greener, safer, and cleaner, students form 5 specialized explorer teams. Each team has distinct duties:

1. Water Watchers (जल प्रहरी)

Core Duty: Checking all drinking water taps, tanks, and handwashing points across the school.

  • Inspect leaking taps and immediately report to the school plumber or caretaker.
  • Promote water conservation during lunch and handwash (*Jal Shakti Abhiyan*).
  • Ensure no water stagnates around taps to prevent mosquito breeding.
2. Electricity Savers (ऊर्जा रक्षक)

Core Duty: Monitoring classrooms and corridors to prevent electricity wastage.

  • Turn off lights and fans when students leave for recess, games, or library.
  • Encourage opening windows and curtains to use natural sunlight and air.
  • Report faulty switches, loose wires, or flickering tubelights for safety.
3. Waste Warriors (स्वच्छता सेनानी)

Core Duty: Overseeing waste disposal and promoting the *Swachh Vidyalaya* campaign.

  • Ensure students throw wet waste in Green Bins and dry waste in Blue Bins.
  • Stop littering in classrooms, verandas, and playgrounds.
  • Collect pencil shavings, dry leaves, and fruit peels for the school compost pit.
4. Green Guardians (हरित संरक्षक)

Core Duty: Nurturing plants, gardens, and protecting nature on campus.

  • Water potted plants, flowering beds, and trees regularly.
  • Observe and protect visiting birds, butterflies, and squirrels.
  • Organize tree-planting (Van Mahotsav) drives with teachers.
5. Traffic Trackers (यातायात निरीक्षक)

Core Duty: Promoting gate safety, queue discipline, and road safety rules.

  • Monitor safe arrival and dispersal of students at the school main gate.
  • Encourage students to use the pedestrian footpath and Zebra Crossing.
  • Help school vans and buses park in orderly lines.

The Green School Concept (हरित विद्यालय की संकल्पना)

Eco-Friendly School

What is a Green School? (हरित विद्यालय क्या है?)

A Green School is an educational institution that actively protects the natural environment by conserving water, minimizing electricity usage, managing waste scientifically, and creating rich green open spaces with native trees and plants.

Say NO to Single-Use Plastic: Replace plastic banners, bottles, and polythene with cloth, paper, and steel tiffins.
Rainwater Harvesting & Jal Shakti: Collecting rooftop rainwater to recharge groundwater aquifers.
Compost Pit (खाद गड्ढा): Converting fallen autumn leaves and fruit peels into rich organic manure for the school garden.
Feature (सुविधा) Ordinary School Green School (Eco-friendly)
Water Usage Leaking taps, overflow ignored Prompt repairs, water recycling
Electricity Fans/lights left ON in empty rooms Switched OFF, natural daylight used
Waste Mixed in one bin, plastic burnt Segregated (Green/Blue), composted
Campus Bare concrete ground, few plants Lush green trees, birds, flowers

Waste Segregation: Green Bin vs. Blue Bin (कचरा पृथक्करण)

Swachh Vidyalaya Practice

Throwing all garbage together makes recycling impossible. Proper waste segregation into color-coded dustbins is essential:

GREEN BIN — Wet Waste (गीला कचरा)

Biodegradable

Natural organic waste that easily decomposes and turns into fertile compost:

✓ Fruit & vegetable peels (छिलके)
✓ Leftover tiffin food (बचा खाना)
✓ Fallen garden leaves & flowers
✓ Tea leaves & eggshells

BLUE BIN — Dry Waste (सूखा कचरा)

Recyclable

Non-biodegradable, clean waste that can be recycled and processed into new goods:

✓ Old newspapers & notebook pages
✓ Cardboard boxes & cartons
✓ Plastic bottles & containers
✓ Glass bottles & metal foil/cans

Textbook Sorting Challenge: Where do these items belong?

Fruit peels, plastic bottle, broken glass, vegetable scraps, old newspaper, dry battery cells, garden leaves, cardboard box.

Correct Sorting:
• Green Bin (Wet): Fruit peels, vegetable scraps, garden leaves.
• Blue Bin (Dry/Recycle): Plastic bottle, broken glass, old newspaper, cardboard box.
• Special Note: Dry battery cells are hazardous e-waste and should go to a designated chemical/battery box!

Textbook Writing Section Solutions (पृष्ठ 57)

Complete Solutions

Question 1: What do I really like about my school?

(मुझे अपने विद्यालय में सबसे अच्छा क्या लगता है?)

Sample Student Answer:

I really like my school because it is a safe, cheerful, and welcoming place. I love:

  • Our kind and encouraging teachers who explain every concept patiently and lovingly.
  • The spacious, well-lit classrooms and the peaceful school library filled with fascinating storybooks.
  • The large playground where my friends and I play football, cricket, and enjoy running.
  • The green trees, flower beds, and clean drinking water facilities.

Question 2: How can I make my school greener?

(मैं अपने विद्यालय को अधिक हरा-भरा कैसे बना सकता/सकती हूँ?)

Sample Student Answer:

I can make my school greener by taking simple, daily actions:

  1. Planting flowering saplings on birthdays and watering potted plants daily.
  2. Always switching off lights and fans before leaving the classroom.
  3. Reporting leaking taps immediately to avoid water wastage.
  4. Separating wet food waste and paper scraps into the correct dustbins.
  5. Saying NO to plastic covers and carrying a reusable steel water bottle and lunchbox.

Question 3: In case there is any difficulty at school, whom do I talk to or inform?

(यदि विद्यालय में कोई कठिनाई या समस्या हो, तो मैं किससे बात करूँ या किसे सूचित करूँ?)

Sample Student Answer:

Whenever I face any difficulty (such as feeling unwell, being bullied, losing an item, or finding school work difficult), I should immediately talk to my Class Teacher. If the issue is serious, I can also inform the School Principal, the School Counselor, or discuss it with my parents at home. We should never keep our problems hidden!

Objective Practice: Fill in the Blanks & True/False

Solved Exercises

A Fill in the Blanks with Appropriate Words:

1. A _______ school takes care of the natural environment.

Answer: green

2. The _______ team checks for leaking taps and water wastage.

Answer: Water Watchers

3. Fruit peels and food scraps are thrown into the _______ bin.

Answer: green (wet waste)

4. Dry and recyclable items like newspapers go into the _______ bin.

Answer: blue (dry waste)

5. _______ Abhiyan is a national campaign focused on saving and protecting water.

Answer: Jal Shakti

B State Whether True or False:

1. We should throw old newspapers into the green dustbin.

Reason: Newspapers are dry recyclable paper, so they belong in the blue bin, not the green bin.

FALSE

2. Wet and dry waste should always be kept in separate bins.

Reason: Separating waste at source allows wet waste to become compost and dry waste to be recycled.

TRUE

3. During a fire emergency, we should panic, scream, and run into the bathroom.

Reason: Bathrooms can trap smoke. We must crawl low under smoke, cover our mouth, and evacuate calmly.

FALSE

4. Trees on the school campus provide cool shade, fresh oxygen, and shelter for birds.

Reason: Greenery significantly improves air quality, lowers temperature, and fosters biodiversity.

TRUE

Multiple Choice Questions (बहुविकल्पीय प्रश्न)

Exam MCQs

1. Which one of the following actions is harmful to the school environment?

(a) Planting more flowering trees
(b) Fixing leaking water taps
(c) Using single-use plastic decorations
(d) Separating wet and dry waste
Correct Answer: (c) — Plastic decorations generate non-biodegradable waste.

2. A school decides to save water, reduce electricity use, and plant more trees. What kind of school is it becoming?

(a) Busy School
(b) Green School
(c) Industrial School
(d) Expensive School
Correct Answer: (b) — Green schools focus on sustainability and eco-friendliness.

3. What is the best way to handle old discarded newspapers and notebooks in school?

(a) Burn them behind the playground
(b) Collect in Blue Bin and give to paper recyclers
(c) Tear and scatter on the grass
(d) Throw them into the compost pit
Correct Answer: (b) — Paper recycling saves trees and prevents air pollution from burning.

4. Which student team is responsible for monitoring lights and fans left on in empty rooms?

(a) Water Watchers
(b) Electricity Savers
(c) Green Guardians
(d) Traffic Trackers
Correct Answer: (b) — Electricity Savers conserve energy.

Important Short & Long Answer Questions (महत्वपूर्ण प्रश्नोत्तर)

Descriptive Bank

Q1. What should you do if you notice a student scribbling or drawing graffiti on classroom desks or walls?

Answer: School property belongs to all of us. If I see anyone scribbling on a bench or wall, I will politely explain to them that defacing school property makes our classroom look untidy. I will request them to erase it and use drawing sheets instead. If they refuse to listen, I will inform our class teacher.

Q2. Explain what emergency safety steps students must follow in case of a fire or earthquake drill.

Fire Safety Rules:

  • Do NOT run or push others; walk briskly in an orderly line towards the open ground.
  • Stay low to the floor (crawl) because smoke rises upward.
  • Cover nose and mouth with a damp handkerchief to avoid breathing toxic fumes.

Earthquake Drill (Drop, Cover & Hold):

  • Drop to the ground under a sturdy classroom desk or table.
  • Cover your head and neck with your arms. Hold onto the desk legs until tremors stop.
  • Stay away from glass windows and tall bookcases.

Q3. Describe the significance of the *Swachh Bharat Swachh Vidyalaya* campaign in daily school life.

Answer: The Swachh Vidyalaya campaign teaches children lifelong habits of hygiene and civic responsibility. It emphasizes:
  • Proper handwashing with soap before eating mid-day meals and after using washrooms.
  • Keeping school grounds clean and free from plastic wrappers and stagnant water.
  • Promoting dignity of labor by encouraging students to keep their own desks and classroom neat.
